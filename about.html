About Mamu

MAMU

A Taste of Filipino Heritage Abroad

Founded by Chef Reinier, a celebrated chef with over 27 years of culinary expertise, MAMU is more than just a food business; it’s a tribute to Filipino heritage, family, and the comfort of home-cooked meals. The story of MAMU started in Quezon City, Philippines, where Chef Reinier’s grandmother, lovingly called "Mamu," began a humble household business. With passion and perseverance, she grew it into one of the top canteens in the area, inspiring Chef Reinier to pursue his culinary dreams. Deeply rooted in Filipino culture, where hospitality and warmth are central values, MAMU aims to provide an unmatched homegrown food experience for Filipinos abroad, particularly those in the Middle East.

In 2017, Chef Reinier brought MAMU to Doha, introducing “Kabayan Nights,” a special offering for Filipinos working far from home. Through dishes that evoke memories of the Philippines, MAMU brings comfort and familiarity to the Filipino community, offering an affordable, “home away from home” experience with every bite.
